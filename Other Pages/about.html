About This Site

A small corner of the internet dedicated to the wonders of our cosmic neighborhood.

What You'll Find Here

Every page in the Planets section covers one world in our solar system — from the Sun to Neptune, plus our Moon. Each one includes quick facts, a short description, fun facts, and an interactive statistics panel with gravity, distance, moons, temperature, and atmosphere composition charts you can compare against every other body.

Why Solar System?

Space is full of amazing, surprising facts — Venus spins backward, Saturn could float in water, and Neptune was discovered with math before it was ever seen. This site is a fun way to learn about the worlds that share our solar system and to compare them side by side.
