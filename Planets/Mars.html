All About Mars 🛰️

Quick Facts

    Where is Mars

    Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, orbiting about 228 million kilometers away, and is often called the "Red Planet".

    More Information

    Mars is a cold, rocky desert world whose reddish color comes from iron oxide dust. It hosts the tallest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, and has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.

    Fun Facts

    Olympus Mons on Mars is about 21 kilometers tall, roughly 2.5 times the height of Mount Everest, and Mars experiences the largest dust storms in the solar system.
