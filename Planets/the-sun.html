All About The Sun 🌞

Quick Facts

    Where is The Sun

    The Sun is located at the center of our solar system, about 150 million kilometers from Earth, and all planets orbit around it.

    More Information

    The Sun is a massive star made of hot plasma that produces light and heat through nuclear fusion, converting hydrogen into helium in its core.

    Fun Facts

    The Sun holds 99.86% of the entire solar system's mass, and its core reaches a scorching 15 million degrees Celsius.
