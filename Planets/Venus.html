All About Venus

Quick Facts

  • Distance from the Sun: about 108.2 million km
  • Day Length: 243 Earth days
  • Year Length: 225 Earth days
  • Moons: 0
  • Temperature: about 465 °C

Where is Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun, orbiting about 108 million kilometers away, and is often called Earth's "sister planet" due to their similar size and mass.

More Information

Venus has a thick atmosphere of carbon dioxide with clouds of sulfuric acid, causing a runaway greenhouse effect and surface temperatures hot enough to melt lead.

Fun Facts

Venus spins backward, so the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east, and a single day there is longer than its entire year.
