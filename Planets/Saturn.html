All About Saturn

Quick Facts

    Where is Saturn

    Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun, orbiting about 1.4 billion kilometers away, and is known for its prominent ring system.

    More Information

    Saturn is a gas giant with a diameter about nine times that of Earth, making it the second-largest planet in our solar system. Its spectacular rings are made of countless chunks of ice and rock.

    Fun Facts

    Saturn has 274 confirmed moons, is so light that it could float in water, and its moon Titan has rivers and lakes made of liquid methane.
