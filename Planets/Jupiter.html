All About Jupiter 🌀

Quick Facts

  • Distance from the Sun: about 778.5 million km
  • Day Length: about 9.9 hours
  • Year Length: about 11.9 Earth years
  • Moons: 101
  • Temperature: about -110 °C

Where is Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun, orbiting about 778 million kilometers away, and is the largest planet in our solar system.

More Information

Jupiter is a gas giant with the Great Red Spot, a raging storm larger than Earth that has been spinning for centuries, and it has 101 officially recognized moons.

Fun Facts

Jupiter has a diameter of about 142,984 kilometers, roughly 11 times the size of Earth, and spins so fast that a single day lasts under 10 hours.
