All About Uranus 🌀

Quick Facts

    Where is Uranus

    Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun, orbiting about 2.9 billion kilometers away, and is known for its unique sideways rotation.

    More Information

    Uranus is an ice giant with a blue-green color caused by methane in its atmosphere, and it rotates on its side with an axial tilt of about 98 degrees.

    Fun Facts

    Because of its sideways tilt, each pole of Uranus gets about 42 years of continuous sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness, and a year there lasts 84 Earth years.
