All About Neptune 🌌

Quick Facts

  • Distance from the Sun: about 4.5 billion km
  • Day Length: about 16.1 hours
  • Year Length: about 165 Earth years
  • Moons: 16
  • Temperature: about -200 °C

Where is Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun, orbiting about 4.5 billion kilometers away, and is known for its deep blue color.

More Information

Neptune is an ice giant with a thick atmosphere of hydrogen and helium, and traces of methane that give it its striking blue color. It has 16 known moons.

Fun Facts

Neptune has the strongest winds in the solar system, reaching speeds of up to 2,100 kilometers per hour, and it was discovered with mathematics before it was ever seen.
