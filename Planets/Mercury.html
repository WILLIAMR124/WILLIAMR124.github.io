All About Mercury 🪐

Quick Facts

    Where is Mercury?

    Mercury is the smallest and innermost planet in our solar system, orbiting closest to the Sun at about 58 million kilometers away.

    More Information

    Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and has a heavily cratered, rocky surface that closely resembles our Moon.

    Fun Facts:

    A year on Mercury (88 Earth days) is shorter than its day (176 Earth days), and despite being closest to the Sun, it is not the hottest planet.
