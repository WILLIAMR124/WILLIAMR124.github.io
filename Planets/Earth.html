All About Earth 🌍

Quick Facts

  • Distance from the Sun: about 150 million km
  • Day Length: 24 hours
  • Year Length: 365.25 days
  • Moons: 1
  • Temperature: average about 15 °C

Where is Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun, orbiting about 150 million kilometers away, and is the only known planet to harbor life.

More Information

Earth is a rocky planet with a protective atmosphere, liquid water covering about 71% of its surface, and a single large moon that stabilizes its tilt and drives its tides.

Fun Facts

Earth is the only planet in our solar system known to support life, rotates at about 1,670 kilometers per hour at the equator, and is the densest planet in the solar system.
