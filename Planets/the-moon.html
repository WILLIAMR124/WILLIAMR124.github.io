All About The Moon 🌕

Quick Facts

    Where is The Moon

    The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite, orbiting at an average distance of about 384,400 kilometers (238,855 miles) from our planet.

    More Information

    The Moon is known for its cratered surface, created by billions of years of asteroid impacts. Its phases are caused by the changing angle of sunlight as the Moon orbits Earth, and its gravity drives our ocean tides.

    Fun Facts

    The Moon has a diameter of approximately 3,474 kilometers, about one-quarter of Earth's. It is slowly drifting away from Earth at about 3.8 centimeters per year, and Apollo astronauts left footprints there that could last millions of years.
